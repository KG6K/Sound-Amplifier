Sound Amplifier is an open-source, copylefted audio loudness booster and sound enhancer designed for Android.

Key Features:
• Intuitive Boost Slider: Amplifies media volume past normal speaker limits up to +60 dB using Android's native LoudnessEnhancer audio effects engine.
• Background Foreground Service: Continues running smoothly across music streaming, podcasts, audiobooks, and video games.
• Quick Settings Tile: Toggle amplification with a single tap directly from your Android notification shade (Android 7.0+).
• Safety Limiter: Visual color transitions (green up to 60%, red above 60%) and dynamic warning notices to protect your device speakers and hearing.
• Broad Android Compatibility: Fully supports Android 6.0 Marshmallow (API 23) through Android 15 (API 35).
• 100% Free & Open Source: No advertisements, no trackers, no in-app purchases, and zero proprietary dependencies.
