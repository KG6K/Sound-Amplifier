Sound Amplifier is an open-source, copylefted audio loudness booster for Android.

Features:
• Boost & Media Volume Sliders: Adjust target gain from 0% to 100% (up to +60 dB) using Android's built-in LoudnessEnhancer API alongside a system media volume slider.
• Quick Turn On / Turn Off: Pause or restore your last boost level with one tap in the header or notification.
• Foreground Service: Applies LoudnessEnhancer to global audio session 0 and media player sessions while running in the background (actual gain and app compatibility vary by device and Android version).
• Quick Settings Tile (Android 7.0+): Toggles amplification directly on Android 12+ (opening the app on Android 7.0–11) and displays the active level in the subtitle on Android 10+.
• Visual High-Boost Warning: Switches the boost indicator to red above 60% with an on-screen caution notice. High volumes can distort audio or damage speakers and hearing.
• Android 6.0 to 15 Support: Built for API 23 through API 35 with no ads, no trackers, and GPL-3.0 licensing.
